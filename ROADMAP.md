# Roadmap

These are proposed future features, not implemented capabilities or measured benchmarks.

1. **Local OCR on rectified views.** Evaluate Apple's [Vision text recognition](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vnrecognizetextrequest) on macOS and [PaddleOCR](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR) mobile models for portability. Generate overlapping perspective or cubemap views with overscan and adaptive scale. Retain text, confidence, language and spherical polygons; merge duplicates by angular distance, overlap and normalized text. Do not download a large document model by default.
2. **Text and number plate review.** Provide a searchable list, confidence and selection; center the camera and bound FOV to the spherical polygon. OCR alone does not reliably classify number plates: assess a compact plate detector, vehicle context and text rules as signals, review proposals and false negatives, and allow manual additions. Use redaction rather than generative fill for privacy.
3. **Faces and redaction.** Evaluate [Vision face detection](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vndetectfacerectanglesrequest) and [MediaPipe Face Detector](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_detector), including its compact full-range BlazeFace variants; test small and profile faces, seams and poles. Expand masks and destructively apply blur, pixelation or opaque coverage in exported pixels. Remove embedded originals, thumbnails and sensitive metadata in a privacy mode. Human review remains necessary; blur and pixelation do not guarantee anonymity.
4. **Media-independent redaction engine.** Define `regionAt(timestamp)` with spherical polygons and effect parameters; separate detection, tracking, review and frame composition. Version a region manifest. Distinguish editable local projects from irreversible exports and do not embed original pixels in privacy exports.
5. **Efficient 360 video.** Detect at adaptive reduced resolution and cadence, track and review trajectories, then decode and render original frames. Evaluate [ByteTrack](https://github.com/FoundationVision/ByteTrack) for association, Kalman filtering and [OpenCV Lucas–Kanade](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/dc/d6b/group__video__track.html) for propagation. ByteTrack itself detects neither faces nor plates. Re-detect after low confidence, occlusion, object entry or scene cuts. Record IDs, actual timestamps, polygons, confidence and keyframes; bridge cubemap seams in spherical or tangent space. Stream blocks/regions via FFmpeg, examine VideoToolbox, preserve variable frame rate, audio synchronization and reviewed 360 metadata, and remove revealing previews. Check licenses of detectors, weights and FFmpeg builds before distribution.

Foreground instance segmentation candidates and concepts: [Apple WWDC 2023](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10176/) and [Vision foreground instance request](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vngenerateforegroundinstancemaskrequest). MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation is a future portable person-segmentation option, not a generic subject detector; measure the complete runtime and weights and review licensing and small-subject accuracy before adopting it.
